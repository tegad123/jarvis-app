Jarvis Mono

Chat first. Black and white, with one blue that means you. Every component below is the React and Tailwind source in components/, rendered from build/. The brand book is brand-book.md.

Button

Actions Open on its own

The pill every action in Jarvis Mono is drawn with: primary (the blue accent fill with white text, accent-hover when hovered or pressed), secondary (panel-raised with a hairline) and ghost (text only).

Sidebar

Navigation Open on its own

The left column that holds the whole app: the wordmark, a new-channel button, the destinations, the direct line, every channel, and who is signed in.

ConversationRow

Navigation Open on its own

One conversation in the sidebar: its name, who spoke last and what they said, when, and whether it's unread or has gone quiet.

MessageBubble

Chat Open on its own

One turn in the chat: yours in a blue bubble (accent, white text) on the right, Jarvis's in a dark panel bubble on the left, with attachments, the app's buttons and the read-aloud control around it.

SystemLine

Chat Open on its own

A single line from the app itself, outside any bubble: a receipt, a notice, a request for action, or an error.

StepList

Chat Open on its own

What Jarvis is doing right now, one step per tool call, each one checking off as it finishes.

Composer

Chat Open on its own

Where a person talks to Jarvis: a pill with + (attach), the text field, the mic, and send. It grows to hold attachments, and a strip above it reports the voice note's state.

ComputerCard

Agent Open on its own

Jarvis's own web browser inside the chat: a live still of the page, what state it's in, and the controls to let Jarvis continue, take over, or hand it back.

ApprovalCard

Agent Open on its own

A request Jarvis can't act on without a person: what it will do, in readable fields or as a before-and-after, and the decision this person is allowed to make.

SettingsSection

Settings Open on its own

One card on the Settings screen: a title, one plain sentence about what it does and who can see it, its current status, its rows, and its actions.

PairingCode

Settings Open on its own

A one-time code a person types on their Mac to connect the iMessage helper, shown large and copyable, with its expiry and a live "Waiting for your Mac…" until the device appears.

SignIn

Access Open on its own

The sign-in screen: Continue with Google first, a PIN for people on the team's roster, and operator access tucked away at the bottom for support.

TodaySummary

Lists Open on its own

One line above the Activity feed that says what Jarvis did today and whether anything needs you.

ActivityRow

Lists Open on its own

One event in the Activity feed: what kind of thing happened, which skill did it, when, and one plain sentence about it, with technical details behind a chevron.

TaskRow

Lists Open on its own

One task on the shared list: its status (tap to move it along), title, details, who has it, when it's due, how long it's been open, and the owner's tools.

LeadRow

Lists Open on its own

One lead from the open-house tracker: how warm they are, their status, the property and price, and the last thing that happened.

FilterChips

Lists Open on its own

A labelled row of pill chips that narrows a list. "All" comes first, and one chip is selected at a time.

OutboxRow

Lists Open on its own

One mail the outbound boundary caught instead of sending, shown to the operator: who it was addressed to, the subject, when, and the body behind a chevron.

NewChannelSheet

Sheets Open on its own

The sheet for naming a new channel or renaming one: one field, one button.

MembersSheet

Sheets Open on its own

Who is in a channel and, for an owner or admin, who else could be. The roster is the input, never a typed name.

EmptyState

States Open on its own

What a screen shows when there is genuinely nothing there: a quiet icon and one sentence about what normally lives in this place.

ErrorState

States Open on its own

What a screen shows when it couldn't load: which thing is unavailable, the plain reason, the technical detail for whoever is helping, and Try again.
